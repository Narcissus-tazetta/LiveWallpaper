# QuickJS-NG

Version: v0.17.0. Source: https://github.com/quickjs-ng/quickjs/tree/v0.17.0

The unmodified engine sources and their MIT license are in `vendor`. The CLI,
`quickjs-libc`, filesystem modules, and network modules are not linked.

`Bridge.c` exposes only source compilation and bounded JSON-in/JSON-out evaluation.
